Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

ConnectApp is a digital application operated by Dream Ladders, a founder-led collaboration ecosystem operated by Shambudu Rajesh Kumar.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how information is handled when you use ConnectApp.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ConnectApp, the application may process the following information.

Account Information

When you register, ConnectApp may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Account authentication information
  • Account-related identifiers generated by the authentication system

Passwords are handled through the application's authentication provider and are not intended to be stored by ConnectApp as readable/plain-text passwords.

Profile Information

You may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your biography or profile description
  • Profile information associated with your account

Some profile information may be visible to other authenticated ConnectApp users, including when they search for people.

Posts and User Content

You may submit:

  • Text posts
  • Images attached to posts
  • Comments
  • Likes and other interactions with posts

Content that you intentionally publish through community features may be visible to other ConnectApp users according to the application's functionality.

Private Messages

ConnectApp provides private messaging between users.

Private messages may include:

  • Sender and recipient account identifiers
  • Message content
  • Message timestamps

Private messaging is intended for conversations between the participating users. Access to private data is also subject to the application's database access controls and security configuration.

Technical and Usage Information

The underlying services used to operate ConnectApp may process technical information necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and deliver the application, such as network, browser, device, authentication, security, and service-operation information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your ConnectApp account
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide profile and search functionality
  • Display community posts
  • Store and display comments and post interactions
  • Enable private messaging
  • Deliver realtime application updates
  • Provide password-reset and account-security functions
  • Store images uploaded to posts
  • Maintain and secure the application
  • Detect, prevent, or investigate misuse
  • Respond to support or privacy requests
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

3. Third-Party Service Providers

ConnectApp relies on third-party infrastructure and services to operate.

Supabase — authentication, database, storage, and realtime application functionality.

Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of the ConnectApp application.

jsDelivr — delivery of the Supabase JavaScript library used by the application.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective infrastructure and services. Their own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

4. Public and Private Information

ConnectApp contains both community and private features.

Information you intentionally publish as a post, comment, or profile information intended to be visible to other users may be accessible to those users.

Private messages are designed for communication between the participating accounts.

You should avoid publishing passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers, confidential business information, or other highly sensitive information through posts, comments, or messages.

5. Images and Uploaded Content

If you attach an image to a post, the image may be stored using the application's cloud storage infrastructure and made available for display through ConnectApp.

Do not upload content that you do not have the right to use or disclose.

6. Notifications

ConnectApp may use browser notification functionality, where supported and permitted by your browser and device, to notify you about events such as new messages.

You can control browser notification permissions through your browser or device settings.

7. Account Security

ConnectApp uses authentication and cloud infrastructure designed to help protect account and application data.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for:

  • Keeping your account credentials confidential
  • Using a strong password
  • Not sharing your authentication information
  • Logging out of shared or public devices
  • Informing us if you believe your account has been compromised

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate ConnectApp, maintain accounts and records, provide requested functionality, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Requests

You may contact us regarding your personal information, including requests concerning access, correction, account-related information, or deletion where applicable.

Email: dreamladdersservices@gmail.com

We may need sufficient information to verify that a request relates to the relevant account before taking action.

10. Children's Use

ConnectApp is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where applicable law requires parental consent or otherwise restricts such collection.

11. External Services and Links

ConnectApp may depend on or interact with third-party infrastructure and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or availability of independently operated third-party services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ConnectApp's functionality, infrastructure, legal requirements, or data practices change.
